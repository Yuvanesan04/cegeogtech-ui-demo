INTELLECTUAL PROPERTIES

Asia Geopolymer Symposium (AGS 2016) & Malaysia-Indonesia Geopolymer Symposium (MIGS 2016)

Introduction

The AGS 2016 aims to provide an excellent opportunity of knowledge sharing and research ideas exchange in the field of advanced material engineering & technology for a better quality of life to meet future challenges. This conference is opened for all academician, postgraduates and post-doctoral students who are currently studying around the world.

MIGS 2016 is a forum to promote the cooperation and understanding among scholars, industries and engineers from Malaysia, Indonesia, and other parts of the world, focusing on Geopolymer research and application. The 1st symposium was held successfully on May 2014 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia and the 2nd MIGS was held in Surabaya, Indonesia, in August 2015. The theme for this conference is ‘Geopolymer Materials for Future’, expecting delegates from many countries to exchange ideas, findings and expertise, as well as to build up research and expertise network.

Objectives
The objectives of the conference are as follows:
• To increase the involvement of scientist, academician and students in an internationally recognised conference.
• To promote the value of knowledge sharing and networking among scientists from all countries on this fields
• To strengthen the bond among the academic institutions of the countries involved in sharing new ideas and strengthen future research activities.

Conference Scope
Asia Geopolymer Symposium & Malaysia-Indonesia Geopolymer Symposium is the premier forum for the presentation of new advances and research results in the fields of Geopolymer Materials Engineering and Technology. The conference will bring together leading researchers, engineers and scientists in the domain of interest from around the world. Topics of interest for submission include, but are not limited to:

Paper Submission
Please prepare your paper in MS Word according to the following instructions. And submit your papers by email attachments to https://submit.confbay.com by the given deadline. All papers should be no less than 5 and not more than 8 pages in length and must be written in strict accordance with the format. Shorter papers should not be included in the journal.

Publication
All paper will be publish under indexed journal and selected best papers from will be published in international indexed journal.
